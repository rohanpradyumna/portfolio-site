Nobody prepares you for the spreadsheet.

The one where you track your days in the country, your visa status, your options if things go wrong, your backup plans, your backup backup plans.

## The Reality

Being an immigrant founder in the US is a constant calculus:

- **OPT days remaining:** A countdown timer on your dreams
- **H-1B lottery odds:** ~25% if you're lucky
- **O-1 requirements:** "Extraordinary ability" — whatever that means
- **Startup runway:** vs. visa runway

You're playing chess on two boards simultaneously, and losing either game ends both.

## What They Don't Tell You

Here's what surprised me:

### 1. Every Decision Has Immigration Implications

Want to quit your job to work on your startup? Check your visa first. Want to take that contract gig? Make sure it doesn't mess with your status. Want to travel home for a family emergency? Factor in re-entry risks.

### 2. The Timeline Pressure Is Brutal

American founders can fail and try again. Immigrant founders often get one shot. The clock is always ticking, and every pivot has to consider whether you'll still have time to execute.

### 3. It Makes You More Resilient

Here's the flip side: navigating this system makes you better at navigating complex problems. Immigration bureaucracy is harder than most startup challenges. If you can handle USCIS, you can handle VCs.

## The Math

Let me break down the actual numbers I was tracking:

- 365 days OPT post-graduation
- +730 days STEM extension (if applicable)
- ~3 H-1B lottery attempts (if timing works out)
- 6-12 months O-1 processing (if you qualify)

Each transition is a risk point. Each risk point requires a plan B.

## What Helped

A few things that made this easier:

1. **Find a community.** Other immigrant founders get it. Join the group chats.
2. **Talk to a good lawyer early.** Not when you need them — before.
3. **Document everything.** Your achievements, your press, your impact. You'll need it for O-1.
4. **Build in public.** It creates a paper trail of your contributions.

## Why I'm Sharing This

Because I wish someone had told me. Because the "just follow your passion" startup advice doesn't account for visa clocks. Because maybe someone reading this is in the same situation and feeling alone.

You're not. The math is hard, but it's not impossible.
